# Naming Brainstorm

**Date:** 2026-02-15
**Participants:** User, Claude

## Context
The project is currently called "Speed Reader" / "Speed Books" — both feel too generic and literal. We want unique, memorable names for:
1. **The project/platform** (replaces "Speed Books")
2. **The book entity** (replaces "Speed Book" — what do you call a single piece of content?)

## Requirements (established during discussion)
- **Tone:** Warm/Artistic + Modern/Techy blend
- **Naming link:** Platform and content unit should be **paired** — different but thematically connected (like "Webtoon" + "episode")
- **Must not exist** as an established product/brand
- **Core concept the user wants to capture:** "Words that are more than just words"
- Content name should feel **derived** from the platform name (like Twitter → Tweet)

## Round 1: Common Words (All Taken)
Tested: Verve, Prosody, Evoke, Motif, Flux, Lumen, Verse, Echo — **all have major brand conflicts.**

## Round 2: Obscure Words (Mostly Taken)
Tested: Cadenza, Parlore, Strofa, Sigil, Folio, Timbre, Voxle — **all taken.**

## Round 3: Coined Words
Tested: Quoth, Vocade, Inkora, Evoca, Recita, Vivot, Motique, Verbora, Verset, Dictara

### Confirmed Available
- **Animot** (anima + mot) — "words with soul" — **no brand conflicts found**
- **Vivot** (vivo + mot) — "living word"
- **Motique** (mot + unique) — "unique words"
- **Verbora** (verbum + ora) — "words that speak"

### User Reactions
- **Animot**: Kept coming back to this one. Initial concern about similarity to "anime" but the meaning is the strongest fit.
- **Verbora**: Liked the meaning "words that speak" but didn't fully commit.
- **Lex-based names**: Too many existing brands use lex/lax.
- **Brevura**: "meh"
- **General direction**: "Words that are more than just words" — soul, elevation, transformation.

## Decision: Animot → Mot

### Platform: Animot
- **Etymology:** anima (Latin: soul/spirit) + mot (French: word)
- **Meaning:** "Words with soul" — words that are more than just words
- **Pronunciation:** ah-nee-MOH (French stress on last syllable)
- **Why it works:**
  - Captures the core concept perfectly — the platform gives soul to words
  - "Anima" also connects to "animate" — the platform animates words (gives them motion, life, expression)
  - No existing brand conflicts (confirmed via search). Animoto and Animotica exist but are different names.
  - Warm and artistic (Latin/French roots) + modern (short, clean, unique)
- **Anime concern addressed:**
  - Pronunciation "ah-nee-MOH" doesn't sound like "AH-nee-may"
  - "Anim-" prefix is shared by animate, animal, animosity — anime doesn't own it
  - In context (a storytelling/reading platform), no one will confuse it with anime

### Content Unit: Mot
- **Etymology:** French for "word." In English, a "mot" means a witty or expressive remark (as in "bon mot").
- **Derivation:** The content name lives inside the platform name — Ani**mot**
- **Why it works:**
  - Short (3 letters), punchy, elegant
  - Already has meaning in English — an expressive, meaningful saying
  - Natural in sentences:
    - "Read a mot on Animot."
    - "Her latest mot hit different."
    - "Browse trending mots."
    - "She published 3 mots this week."
  - The platform gives soul to words (animot), each piece IS a word-with-soul (mot)

### The Relationship
- **Animot** = the platform that imbues words with soul (anima + mot)
- **Mot** = one piece of content — a word-experience with emotion, pacing, and expression
- The content name is embedded in the platform name, creating a natural linguistic bond
- Parallels: Twitter → Tweet, Snapchat → Snap, Animot → Mot

## Status
**Agreed (for now)** — Animot as platform name, Mot as content unit. Subject to change as the project evolves.
